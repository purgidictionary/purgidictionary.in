What the entries represent

The project is a lexicographical resource developed through field engagement. The documentation aims to preserve vocabulary, expressions, idioms, elements of cultural knowledge and linguistic memory, while providing practical reference value.

The resulting resource is intended to remain open to correction and expansion, making it a living resource rather than a static list of translated words.

How users can search

The dictionary includes an English search facility. Users can begin with an English word and access corresponding Purgi, Hindi and Urdu meanings.

This design helps make Purgi more visible to learners, educators and researchers beyond the immediate speech community while keeping Purgi at the centre.
