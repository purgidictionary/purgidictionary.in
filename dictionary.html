The Purgi Dictionary

The first-ever multilingual digital Purgi Dictionary brings together Purgi, English, Hindi and Urdu in one freely accessible digital resource. Created as an independent social-service initiative, it documents Purgi vocabulary through a collection of 5,000 lexical entries for learners, teachers, researchers and the wider community.

The idea

The Purgi Dictionary began as an independent social-service effort to create a practical, freely accessible language resource for the Purgi-speaking community. Its first edition brings together Purgi, English, Hindi and Urdu, with an English search facility to help users access the corresponding meanings.

The dictionary is deliberately multilingual: Purgi, English, Hindi and Urdu are brought together within one digital resource. An English search facility lets users begin with a widely used language and reach Purgi, Hindi and Urdu meanings.

What the entries represent

The dictionary is a lexicographical resource developed through field engagement and vocabulary documentation. The wider project also seeks to document expressions, idioms, elements of cultural knowledge and linguistic memory wherever these have been recorded.

The resource is intended to remain open to correction and expansion as further vocabulary and community feedback become available.

How users can search

The dictionary includes an English search facility. Users can begin with an English word and access corresponding Purgi, Hindi and Urdu meanings.

This design helps make Purgi more accessible to learners, educators and researchers while keeping Purgi at the centre of the resource.

Created for the community

The work was undertaken as an independent social-service initiative by Dr. Winsy Abraham, growing from language learning, community engagement and field documentation across Kargil.

The project is self-funded through personal resources and has not received grants, donations or external financial assistance. The dictionary is freely accessible as a contribution to Purgi language documentation, learning, teaching and future research.
