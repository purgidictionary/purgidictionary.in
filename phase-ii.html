The underlying principle

The first edition solves an immediate accessibility problem through Latin representation. Phase II can add the Purgi script without abandoning that accessibility. Together, script and transliteration can support vocabulary documentation, literacy, language learning and intergenerational transmission.

The goal is not simply a larger dictionary. It is a living digital resource for Purgi.

Phase II is a proposed continuation of the work; features will be developed progressively according to community needs, verification and technical feasibility.
