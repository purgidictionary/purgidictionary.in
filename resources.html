For researchers and institutions

The Purgi Dictionary may serve as a starting point for linguistic research, educational resource development, translation, creative writing and further documentation of Purgi. It provides a freely accessible multilingual vocabulary resource for researchers, educators, students and others interested in the language.

The proposed Phase II offers opportunities for further collaboration in community verification, Purgi-script integration, pronunciation resources, expanded vocabulary and language-learning materials.
