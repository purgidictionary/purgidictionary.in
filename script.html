Writing & literacy

Purgi Script

The Purgi script represents an important step in the development of written Purgi. To make the first edition of the dictionary immediately accessible to a wider range of readers, Purgi words are presented using the Latin alphabet. This approach helps users explore and learn Purgi vocabulary while recognising the importance of developing literacy in the Purgi script.

6 June 2026

The Purgi script was publicly launched in Kargil on 6 June 2026. This marked an important milestone in the language's written development and opened an opportunity to integrate the script into digital language resources.

Accessibility first

The first edition uses Latin-script representations of Purgi words to support readers who may not yet be familiar with the Purgi script. This is a practical choice for the current edition, not a replacement for Purgi writing.

Next step

Script + transliteration

The proposed Phase II will aim to present Purgi words in both the Purgi script and Latin transliteration. Retaining transliteration alongside the script can support wider access while encouraging familiarity with the written form of the language. The next phase will also provide an opportunity to refine and expand the dictionary with community input.
