The work behind the dictionary

A social-service initiative for Kargil

The Purgi Dictionary was created as a social-service initiative: a practical effort to document Purgi vocabulary and create a freely accessible resource for the people of Kargil, especially learners, teachers and future researchers.

Created and maintained by Dr. Winsy Abraham, the work grew from language learning, community engagement and field documentation across Kargil.

The initiative is independently undertaken and self-funded by Dr. Abraham through personal resources, without grants, donations or external financial assistance.

The creator

Dr. Winsy Abraham

Educator, social worker, lexicographer and former senior Human Resources professional based in Kargil, Ladakh.

From corporate experience to community service

After over 17 years in the corporate sector, Dr. Abraham and his family relocated from Indore to Kargil in October 2021, dedicating themselves to social service.

The Purgi Dictionary is one outcome of this commitment. Developed through direct engagement with local communities and extensive field documentation, it reflects his efforts to document and preserve the linguistic heritage of the region.
