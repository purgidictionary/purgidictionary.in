The creator

Dr. Winsy Abraham

Educator, social worker, lexicographer and former senior Human Resources professional based in Kargil, Ladakh.

From corporate career to Kargil

Dr. Winsy Abraham holds a Ph.D. in the Faculty of Management and an MBA in Human Resources. He brings over 17 years of corporate experience in Human Resources and Management, having worked in the IT industry as a Human Resource Business Partner (HRBP) and Location HR Head. During this period, he gained extensive experience in strategic human resource management, employee engagement, organisational development and people management.

Originally from Bhopal, Dr. Abraham built his corporate career in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. In October 2021, he relocated to Kargil, Ladakh, with his family, choosing to move from the corporate sector towards community development and social service. Since then, his efforts have focused on education, regional development, community engagement, cultural preservation and language documentation, with a particular commitment to the educational and linguistic development of the region.

Dr. Abraham undertakes his social-service and community-development initiatives independently, without representing or working under any NGO or charitable organisation. These initiatives, including the Purgi Dictionary project, are entirely self-funded and supported through his personal resources. He has not received grants, donations or external financial assistance for this work.

The Purgi work

As a learner of Purgi who came to the language from outside the community, Dr. Abraham began his work through direct engagement with speakers and villages across Kargil. Between 2022 and 2025, he travelled approximately 500 km and visited more than 60 villages, documenting vocabulary and observing local usage. This social-service work culminated in the 5,000-entry multilingual digital dictionary, created as a freely accessible resource for the Purgi-speaking community.

About the dictionary

The Purgi Dictionary is a major language-documentation initiative arising from Dr. Abraham's community work in Kargil. Its 2026 digital edition contains 5,000 lexical entries across Purgi, English, Hindi and Urdu and is freely available through Android and iOS applications. The dictionary is also officially listed on Bharatavani, where Dr. Abraham is identified as its author and content partner.

Research reports and policy papers

Authored a 300-page independent research and policy White Paper proposing the Kargil Education Transformation Framework (KETF 2040), a long-term framework for transforming the region's education system. The work draws on NEP 2020, international research and educational practices, and the geographical, linguistic, socio-cultural and institutional realities of Kargil. The White Paper has been submitted to the Kargil District authorities and University Scholars for consideration.
