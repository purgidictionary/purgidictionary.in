The creator

Dr. Winsy Abraham

Educator, Social worker, Lexicographer and former senior Human Resources professional based in Kargil, Ladakh.

From corporate career to Kargil

Dr. Abraham relocated with his family to Kargil in October 2021 after a senior corporate career. His subsequent work has centred on education, community engagement, social service, cultural preservation and language documentation.

Education work in Kargil

  • Guest Faculty, University of Ladakh, Kargil Campus — June to December 2022
  • Principal, Munshi Habibullah Mission School, Kargil — September 2023 to June 2025
  • Voluntary personality-development trainer in government and private schools across Kargil — from June 2025
  • Author of a 300-page White Paper, Vision 2040: A White Paper on Transforming Kargil's Education System, proposing the Kargil Education Transformation Framework (KETF 2040)

The Purgi work

As a non-native learner and lexicographer, Dr. Abraham entered Purgi through direct engagement with speakers and communities. Over approximately four years he travelled nearly 500 km and visited more than 60 villages, collecting vocabulary and observing local usage. This social-service work culminated in the 5,000-entry multilingual digital dictionary, created as a freely accessible resource for the Purgi-speaking community.

About the dictionary

The Purgi Dictionary is a major language-documentation project associated with Dr. Abraham's community work in Kargil. The 2026 digital edition contains 5,000 lexical entries and is freely available through Android and iOS applications. The dictionary is also officially listed on Bharatavani, where Dr. Abraham is identified as its author and content partner.
